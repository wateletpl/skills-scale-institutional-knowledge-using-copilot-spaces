# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead** on test coverage, acceptance criteria validation, and testability requirements
- **Works with Technical Lead/Architect** on design reviews and technical feasibility
- **Works with Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature specifications
- **Supports Project Managers** with effort estimates and risk identification

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Partners with Project Managers** on timelines and release planning
- **Collaborates with UX/Design Lead** on user experience and design requirements
- **Works with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on business priorities and success metrics
- **Provides requirements to Developers** through clear acceptance criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Collaborates with Product Managers** on prioritization and scope management
- **Works with Release Manager** on deployment schedules and readiness
- **Escalates blockers** identified by Developers and QA/Testing Leads
- **Coordinates with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on status and decisions
- **Partners with Security Lead** on security-related risks and compliance requirements

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They work with product and engineering to define testability requirements and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for the project
- Create and maintain acceptance test plans
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate features against acceptance criteria
- Identify and track quality risks and regressions
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test coverage

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce post-release defects through effective testing
- Establish clear quality standards and Definition of Done

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement discussions
- Daily standups with development team
- Test result reports and quality metrics
- Risk reviews and release readiness assessments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works closely with Developers** on test coverage requirements and testability
- **Validates acceptance criteria** defined by Product Managers
- **Reports quality metrics** to Project Managers and identifies testing-related risks
- **Coordinates with Release Manager** on pre-release smoke tests and go/no-go decisions
- **Partners with Security Lead** on security testing and compliance validation

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical design and system architecture for projects. They guide engineering decisions, identify technical risks, and ensure solutions align with long-term system goals.

### Responsibilities
- Drive technical design and architecture decisions
- Review technical proposals and design documents
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure code quality and adherence to standards
- Guide junior developers and support technical mentorship
- Collaborate with product on feasibility and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risks
- Foster a culture of engineering excellence

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and technical discussions
- Code review guidance and architecture documentation
- Technical risk assessments in planning and execution phases
- Coordination with other technical leads on cross-team dependencies

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Mentors and guides Developers** on design decisions and best practices
- **Works with Product Managers** on feasibility assessment and trade-offs
- **Collaborates with Release Manager** on deployment architecture and rollback strategies
- **Identifies technical risks** that Project Managers escalate
- **Partners with Security Lead** on security architecture and threat mitigation

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and foster a culture of continuous improvement. They enable the team to work efficiently and maintain psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile practices and methodologies
- Monitor team velocity and identify process improvements
- Support psychological safety and collaborative decision-making
- Track and communicate process metrics

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and efficiency
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement
- Ensure predictable, sustainable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- One-on-ones with team members
- Process improvement recommendations to Project Manager

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers** to coordinate schedules and communicate impediments
- **Supports all team members** (Developers, QA, Product Managers) in working together smoothly
- **Escalates blockers** from the team to Project Managers for resolution
- **Facilitates collaboration** between Product Managers and technical staff

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are executives or business owners who provide strategic direction, funding, and approval for projects. They ensure projects align with organizational priorities and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Approve projects and significant scope changes
- Supply funding and resource allocation decisions
- Communicate organizational priorities
- Review progress and impact against success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Align product roadmap with organizational strategy
- Maximize return on investment

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and reviews
- Go/no-go decision gates at key milestones
- Email briefings on project status and risks
- Executive-level escalations and approvals

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers** on strategic priorities and success metrics
- **Receives status updates from Project Managers** on progress and risks
- **Makes prioritization decisions** that inform the Product Manager's backlog
- **Provides budget and resource approvals** coordinated through Project Managers

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience, design standards, and usability requirements. They ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs and organizational branding.

### Responsibilities
- Define design standards and user experience approach
- Conduct user research and usability validation
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Review designs for consistency and accessibility
- Collaborate with developers on implementation details
- Identify and track design-related risks

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Ensure consistent branding and design language
- Reduce rework through early validation

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and usability reports
- Design specifications and accessibility guidance
- Collaboration with developers during implementation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Partners with Product Managers** on feature definitions and user needs
- **Works with Developers** on design implementation and feasibility
- **Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead** on usability acceptance criteria
- **Reports design-related risks** to Project Managers
- **Coordinates with Stakeholders/Sponsors** on design strategy and brand alignment

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release schedules, and ensure smooth transitions to production. They own the release process and serve as the primary point of coordination during deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning and deployment schedules
- Prepare and execute deployment checklists
- Manage rollback and contingency plans
- Verify production deployment success
- Communicate release status to stakeholders
- Track and manage release dependencies

### Goals
- Execute reliable, low-risk deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents
- Maintain clear communication during releases

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and coordination meetings
- Deployment status updates and notifications
- Post-deployment verification reports
- Rollback and incident communication

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers** on release scheduling and go/no-go decisions
- **Coordinates with Developers** on deployment readiness and rollback procedures
- **Partners with QA/Testing Lead** on pre-release smoke tests and acceptance validation
- **Collaborates with Technical Lead/Architect** on deployment architecture and contingencies
- **Updates Stakeholders/Sponsors** on release status and business impact
- **Coordinates with Security Lead** on security-related deployment requirements

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that projects meet security requirements, compliance standards, and threat mitigation protocols. They work proactively to identify and address security risks throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Review code, architecture, and deployment for security
- Identify and track security-related risks
- Ensure security scanning is integrated into CI/CD
- Provide security guidance and best practices training

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant solutions
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and breaches
- Build security-aware team culture

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design discussions
- Vulnerability reports and risk assessments
- Security incident communication and response
- Compliance and audit documentation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Advises Developers** on secure coding practices and vulnerability prevention
- **Reviews designs with Technical Lead/Architect** for security implications
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead** on security testing and compliance validation
- **Identifies security risks** escalated by Project Managers
- **Collaborates with Release Manager** on secure deployment procedures
- **Updates Stakeholders/Sponsors** on security posture and regulatory compliance
